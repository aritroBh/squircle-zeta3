**How to read the tables.** Table 1 has one row per theorem-like environment of `main.tex`, in order of appearance (44 environments: 1 lemma in the text, 31 theorems, 11 propositions, 1 lemma in Appendix A; the paper has no corollary, and its one "conjecture" environment, label `conj:field`, is printed as Theorem 18). Printed numbers and pages come from `paper/ieee/core/scripts/full/main.aux.draft44`, the aux file of the build of this exact `main.tex` (theorems, propositions and lemmas have three separate counters). "Table II status" quotes the status cell of the matching row of the paper's Table II (`tab:verify`, begins on p. 39; `R<n>` is the row number counting the three parts of the table as one list, 1 to 107), or says "no row". "Proof" is where the full written proof or derivation lives; a `result.md` is the job's report, a `proof*.md` the written proof. "Referee" is an independent report (another agent, own code) with its verdict in three words; "none found" means no referee report file exists. "Scripts" are the main ones to re-run (each is a `verify*`/`check*`/`planner_check*` script, or the engine that produced the numbers); a note in brackets names a section of the multi-section checkers `paper/ieee/facts_check.py`, `paper/ieee/extra_checks.py`, `paper/ieee/review_math.py`. Table 2 gives, in the same columns, one line per Table II row that cites no theorem-like environment in its Result cell. Section 3 lists the † results with no independent referee report. Folder names are reliable. `paper/ieee/DAGGER_INVENTORY.md` was synced to draft 44 on the same day. The Proof column also names the section file of the proofs supplement (`paper/ieee/proofs/sec_*.tex`; 38 sections, `paper/ieee/proofs/supplement.pdf`, 309 pp., covering all 35 daggered theorems and propositions) and the Referee column its referee reports (`paper/ieee/proofs/referee_pilot/`, `paper/ieee/proofs/referee_g1/` to `paper/ieee/proofs/referee_g4/`, `paper/ieee/proofs/referee_g8/`, `referee_g8bc/`, `referee_g9/`, `paper/ieee/proofs/referee_unread/`). `paper/ieee/proofs/AUDIT_RECHECK.md` says which independent pass read which text of the supplement. Results with no section in the supplement have none named (the supplement has no section for any Table II row without a theorem number). "Refereed" and "referee report" mean an independent AI-model re-check with its own code, not journal peer review.
