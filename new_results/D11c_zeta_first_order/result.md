## 4. THEOREM 3 (the ζ(n)-tail: coefficient of ζ(n) in a_N for all N ≥ n) — PROVED, sympy-verified to N=12

## 5. THEOREM 4 (geometric formula for ζ(3)) — PROVED, 70-digit verification; honesty: representation equivalent to Euler's classical integral, the *geometry* is what's new

**ζ(3) = π²/3 + P⁗(1)/(6√2)**, P(p) = perimeter of {|x|^p+|y|^p=1}.

**Derivation.** a₃ = P‴(1)/(3!√2) and a₃ = ζ(3)−π²/3. Writing P(p) = 4(1/p)G(u), u=1/p−1, G(u)=∫₀¹√((1−t)^{2u}+t^{2u})dt, the chain rule (u′=−1, u″=2, u‴=−6 at p=1) gives P‴(1) = −4G‴(0) − 36G″(0) − 48G′(0). Differentiating under the (integrably log-singular) integral and using ∫lnS=−2, ∫l²=π²/3, ∫ln²S=8−π²/3, ∫ln³S=−12+6J, ∫lnS·l²=−12−2J (S=t(1−t), l=ln(t/(1−t)), J:=∫₀¹ln²t ln(1−t)dt):

P‴(1)/√2 = 18 + 3J − 3π²   ⇒   **ζ(3) = 3 − π²/6 + ½∫₀¹ ln²(t) ln(1−t) dt**.

Equivalent forms (all verified): ζ(3) = 3 − π²/6 − ½∫₀¹∫₀¹ t ln²t/(1−t+ts) ds dt (double integral); ζ(3) = 3 − π²/6 + ¼∫₀¹ ln t ln(1−t) ln(t(1−t)) dt (symmetric); and P‴(1)/(√2) as the explicit single log-integral ∫₀¹[−½ln³S − 3lnS·l² − 9ln²S − 18l² − 24lnS]dt.

**50-digit checks (mpmath, 70 dps, step_06):** (i) perimeter form: error 7.1e-70; (ii) J-form: 1.8e-71; (iv) double integral: 1.8e-71; (v) symmetric: 1.8e-71; (vi) independent 9-point FD of direct arclength quadrature: P‴(1) = −17.7156657173 vs 6√2(ζ(3)−π²/3) = −17.7156657474.

**Novelty (honest):** J = −2Σ_{k≥1} 1/(k(k+1)³) = 2ζ(3) − 6 + π²/3 is Euler's classical beta-integral evaluation (verified to 30+ digits), so the simplified integral formula is *equivalent to a classical representation* — it is **not** a new way to compute ζ(3), and it does not reduce to ∫₀¹ln²t/(1−t)dt = 2ζ(3) or Beukers' integral (both checked to be numerically different objects). The genuinely new content is the *geometric realization*: ζ(3)−π²/3 is 1/(6√2) times the third derivative of the superellipse perimeter at the diamond, and more generally ζ(n) first enters at dⁿ with the closed-form coefficient κ_n of Theorem 1.
